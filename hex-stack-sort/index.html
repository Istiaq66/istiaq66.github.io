How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down.

  1. Pick a stack from the tray and place it on an empty hex.
  2. Neighboring stacks with the same top color slide together.
  3. Get 10 or more of one color on top to clear them.
  4. Fill the progress bar to win, and don't let the board fill up!
