Hex Stack Sort

A relaxing hex-tile puzzle. Stack, merge and sort.

Coming soon on Google Play

How to play

  • Pick a stack from the tray and place it on an empty hex.
  • Neighboring stacks with the same top color slide together.
  • Get 10 or more of one color on top to clear them.
  • Fill the progress bar to win the level, and don't let the board fill up.

Features

  • Endless levels with new colors and board shapes as you go
  • A guided tutorial to learn the basics in seconds
  • Daily rewards, 12 achievements and coin rewards
  • Play offline, with no account needed
