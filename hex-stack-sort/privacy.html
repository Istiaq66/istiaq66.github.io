Privacy Policy: Hex Stack Sort

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Hex Stack Sort (the "App"), published by IS-Tech ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at istiaq.ahmed@gtaf.org.

Summary

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your real name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device (and in your own Google device backup, if you use one). It is never sent to us.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device so you can keep playing: coins, level progress, game statistics (for example tiles cleared and levels won), achievements, daily reward streak, sound/music/vibration settings, and the nickname and avatar you choose in your profile.

This information is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup (stored by Google for you) so your progress can be restored on a new phone. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings deletes it from your device. Your nickname is a name you make up; please don't use personal information in it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional "rewarded" ads you can choose to watch for in-game coins. To provide ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example ads shown and tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information to deliver, personalize (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you are in a region that requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or certain US states), the App asks for your consent choices through Google's consent tool before showing personalized ads. You can change your choices later in the App under Profile → Settings → Privacy options (shown when your region requires it).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Google Play Games (optional)

The App may offer online leaderboards and achievements through Google Play Games Services. If you choose to sign in, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and player ID) and your submitted scores under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

4. Information we don't collect

We don't run our own servers for the App, and we don't use analytics tools. We don't receive your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location from the App. We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device (and in your own device backup, if enabled) until you delete it. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled and retained by Google according to its policies and is transmitted using encryption.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold your game data on our servers, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For advertising data, use the choices in section 2 or contact Google. For any request or question, email istiaq.ahmed@gtaf.org.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. We'll post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
